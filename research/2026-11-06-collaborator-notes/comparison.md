# Comparison with the existing project

Date: November 6, 2026.

This comparison concerns the unchanged supplied documents and the existing Version 4 manuscript. The [height replacement](height-replacement.md) records the one adopted transfer from the current project into these notes. The other ideas below remain research or independently stated reductions.

## Useful additions

The equivalence note needs only a fixed existential predicate accepting every integer and excluding negative odd valuations outside one fixed finite prime set. It supplies denominator-clearing conditions which use integer completeness of that predicate. For example, forcing a denominator to have the form `1+rW` makes it a unit at the relevant odd-contact primes. The selected-place construction uses the same root data throughout its local comparisons. These are useful explicit interfaces for the arithmetic predicate.

Section 12 of the arithmetic-input note separates the rational-point conclusion from the finite descent. Under its precise full-two-torsion and three-dimensional Selmer hypotheses, the possibilities are rank one with trivial two-primary Sha, or rank zero with two-primary Sha isomorphic to Q_2/Z_2. Both have full two-primary Selmer corank one. Excluding the divisible Sha alternative is an arithmetic obligation, not an automatic consequence of the dimension calculation. The note does not certify or disprove the existing restricted-converse proof.

Sections 10 and 13 express the paired-point problem concretely. For positive nonsquare beta, put

\[
k_\beta(s)=\frac{\beta(s^2-\beta)}{s^2+3\beta},\qquad k_\beta(\infty)=\beta.
\]

A nonzero x-coordinate of squareclass beta on `y^2=x(x-e)(x+3e)` is equivalent to `e=z^2 k_beta(s)` with nonzero rational z and projective rational s. The paired condition is `theta k_beta(s)=u^2 k_beta(t)`. For finite parameters it becomes

\[
V^2=\theta(s^2-\beta)(s^2+3\beta)(t^2-\beta)(t^2+3\beta),\quad V\ne0.
\]

The projective version retains infinity and the permitted nonzero two-torsion points. The algebra has been checked independently. Neither this equation nor positive rank of a related Jacobian establishes the required torsor's rational solubility.

The reduced-input article uses a large fixed multiple of the elliptic generator to make a comparison constant small, and introduces index variables that make the Lucas relations quadratic. Its precision K=24 can be preserved under the height replacement. Extra index points and the fixed multiplication map have their own circuit cost. No improved quartic count follows without constructing and reviewing that circuit.

## Shortcuts which do not follow

The norm identity in Section 13 of the arithmetic-input note does not alone provide both points. Its example with B=17, U=1, H=272 disproves that shortcut, but fails the preparation's additional square conditions, so it is not a counterexample to the full prepared construction.

The proposed congruent-number CM replacement has a norm obstruction for its specified Kummer classes. This does not rule out every CM construction. The restricted-contact argument also shows why a radical counting only primes inert in a fixed finite set of quadratic fields cannot replace the full contact radical. The compact quaternionic family cannot simply be replaced by a nonisotrivial elliptic scheme on the same proper base.

## Lower bounds

The separate note argues for total degree at least four, at least two witnesses in general, and at least four witnesses for a globally nonnegative quartic. Its stronger conclusions also concern joint witness degree, irreducibility, monomials and circuit gates. The cubic-unirationality input was checked against Kollár's Theorem 2, and the one-witness obstruction was checked against the cited existential-definability literature. A complete independent review of all the stronger arguments remains outstanding. The note makes no claim that its boundary constructions exist.
