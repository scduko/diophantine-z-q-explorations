# Routes toward a genuinely simpler proof

## 1. A master-lemma paper without an expanded polynomial

**Feasibility: high. New mathematics: low. Expository value: high.**

State a finite quadratic encoding lemma, prove soundness and completeness
arithmetically, and invoke sum-of-squares compression once. Put source
code, tables, eliminations and audits in a supplement. This can make the
central proof dramatically shorter while remaining rigorous.

## 2. Primitive divisors of one elliptic divisibility sequence

**Feasibility: speculative. Potential payoff: very high.**

Elliptic divisibility sequences automatically acquire primitive prime
divisors. Seek one index whose denominator supplies all local tests, with
primitive-divisor growth replacing the simultaneous Gaussian prime pattern.

The missing lemma must give a large enough radical and automatic avoidance
of the translated denominator needed for the second observation. Primitive
divisor existence alone does not provide existential uniformity or the
required prime separation.

## 3. One global defect instead of contact detectors

**Feasibility: medium-low. Potential payoff: high.**

The odd-product identity already turns four point heights into one height.
Search for a rational function \(G\) on the fiber product such that every
nonzero comparison defect divides one value of \(G\), while \(h(G)\) is
globally controlled. A product formula or resultant might then replace
the three detector copies and their pole equations.

The obstacle is that integer divisibility is local whereas a rational
function supplies one global equation. The detectors bridge that gap.

## 4. Quaternionic or norm-form definitions of integrality

**Feasibility: low as a pure existential shortcut.**

Norm forms give elegant semilocal rings and universal definitions of
\(\mathbb Z\) in \(\mathbb Q\). They do not presently turn the intersection
over all primes into one existential formula. Treating logical negation
or a universal quantifier as existential would simply assume the result.

## 5. A single geometric fiber product

**Feasibility: medium as exposition, uncertain as simplification.**

Package the construction as a variety \(V\to\mathbb A^1\) with
\[
\pi(V(\mathbb Q))=\mathbb Z.
\]
Separate the label variety, local-comparison correspondence and height
detector as three fiber products. This is a clean way to search for a
direct map or a redundant factor, though it does not itself prove the
projection statement.

## 6. Universal-polynomial compression

**Feasibility: circular at the defining stage.**

Once integer witnesses are available, universal Diophantine polynomials
can compress computations. Using them to define the integers over
\(\mathbb Q\) assumes that their witnesses are integers, which is the
point being proved.

## 7. Canonical heights alone

**Feasibility: low without new algebraic encoding.**

The identity \(\hat h([n]P)=n^2\hat h(P)\) makes the labels transparent,
but real height inequalities are not existential polynomial equations.
The current radical machinery is what converts height information into
algebraic constraints.

## Recommended split

Pursue three tracks:

1. write the short existential proof around a master proposition;
2. recast the source as a small geometric fiber product and seek one
   global defect;
3. test whether elliptic primitive divisors can replace simultaneous
   contact primes while preserving the shifted comparison.

Circuit optimization should remain a separate finite track. It can improve
the record polynomial but will not by itself yield a conceptual proof.
