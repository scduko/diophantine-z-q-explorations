\paragraph{Version convention for the Tao--Ziegler references.}
The theorem and equation numbers used here for Tao and Ziegler,
\emph{Polynomial patterns in the primes}, refer to
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.07817v1}{arXiv:1603.07817v1}.
The corresponding numbers in the published version,
\emph{Forum of Mathematics, Pi} \textbf{6} (2018), e1,
\href{https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2017.3}{doi:10.1017/fmp.2017.3},
are as follows.
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{arXiv:1603.07817v1}&\text{published version}\\\hline
\text{Theorem 1.4}&\text{Theorem 4}\\
\text{Theorem 2.5}&\text{Theorem 9}\\
\text{Theorem 3.1}&\text{Theorem 11}\\
\text{Corollary 3.9}&\text{Corollary 3}\\
\text{Theorem 4.1}&\text{Theorem 13}\\
\text{equation (3.5)}&\text{equation (36)}\\
\text{equation (4.1)}&\text{equation (57)}
\end{array}
\]
The arbitrary-function part of Theorem 3.1 and Corollary 3.9 are used
for the Artin-restricted weights. The special conclusion with constant
dense model for the ordinary von Mangoldt function is not applied to
those weights.
