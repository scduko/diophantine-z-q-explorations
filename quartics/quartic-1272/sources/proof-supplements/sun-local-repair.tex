In either chart, $t\mid R(x,z,w)$, where
$R(x,z,w)=\kappa w^2-xz$.
Fix
\[
 S_R=\{p:\ p\text{ is prime and }p\mid 2\kappa\}.
\]
For every prime outside $S_R$, the ternary form $R$ retains rank three
and the prime is odd. Let
\[
 q=\prod_{\substack{p\mid t\\p\notin S_R}}p.
\]
Thus $q$ is a squarefree product of good primes, $q\mid t$, and $q\ll B$.
For each fixed $q$, the number of possible nonzero $t$ with
$|t|\ll B$ is $O_{\kappa,\alpha}(B^\alpha)$.
Indeed, $t/q$ is supported on primes dividing $q$ or belonging to $S_R$,
so the Rankin bound in the original proof applies with this fixed set.
