In particular Qi and P* are themselves in 2E(K). A further doubling is not needed to put either target into the finite doubling image. This observation is specific to the current source conventions; it must not be applied to an arbitrary halfpoint carrier.

## Soundness

Set V0=H−P*, Vi=Ui−Qi. The existing subtraction guards exclude coincident abscissae, hence h=0 or 1 cannot occur on a valid source solution. All these differences are finite nonidentity points of 2E(K), so the inherited point-domain and logarithm lemmas still apply.

At a consumed prime p>76176 the primary and conjugate formal-logarithm ratios are

    ri=ui/h,       ri'=(ui−ni)/(h−1).

At least one of h,h−1 is a p-unit. Its ratio is integral because ui,ni,h are integers. The relative-error lemma and the cross test make both truncated ratios integral; both approximation errors are then absolute errors at order ki. Thus Li is congruent to ri and ri' modulo p^ki, with ki=12 for beta and 40 for the other labels. Multiplication by the integer denominators and subtraction give

    h Li=ui,       (h−1)Li=ui−ni  modulo p^ki,

The inherited theorem gives v2(d−)=6, v2(d+)=2, gcd(d−(p),d+(q))=4 for every pair of selected primes, and avoidance of every odd prime factor of L.
