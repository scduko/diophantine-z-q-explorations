# Included research and limits

Version 5 includes the 1,272-coordinate quartic manuscript, complete source and coefficient tables, transformation certificates, independent checks, source reviews and dependency inventory. These files are under `current/quartics/quartic-1272/` and `current/docs/quartic-1272/`. The current source uses comparison orders 16 and 52 and scalar order 32. The release descriptor and package manifest pin the exact files and completed reproduction receipt.

The Version 4 manuscript and package, earlier quartics, and original construction remain at their existing paths. The following paragraphs describe the preserved Version 4 and earlier collection, with its historical counts and qualifications.

## Historical Version 4 collection

The preserved collection includes the exact recorded public Git snapshot, the explicit
Version 4 history selection, inherited selected Version 3 and Version 2 research,
and, in the full archive, the unchanged public November 4 revision-2 baseline.
The current-only archive supplies the baseline URL and checksum instead.

The Version 4 result is the 4,973-variable quartic, with 4,972 witnesses,
72,925 monomials, degree four and maximum coefficient magnitude
685783949153203534233890. The original huge polynomial is also retained in
the manuscript. The source generation, exact polynomial, finite certificate,
source audit, reproduction runner and dependencies are under
`current/quartics/quartic-4973/`.

`current/docs/quartic-4973/` contains the final theorem inventory and explicit
source-version qualifications. `current/docs/quartic-4973-validation/` records
the final integration review, finite-rule review and fresh reproduction.
The complete current snapshot also includes the retained partial Lean helper
snapshot. It does not contain a completed formal proof of the main theorem.

`history/version4/PROOF_INDEX.json` maps the earlier frozen proof/review IDs to
included records. `history/version4/release-research/` supplies the later
first-norm source reviews, revision-3 appendix reviews, denominator clarification
and final integration review. Earlier status words such as pending describe
the old artifact, not the final release. Optional scalar/J0 fusion remains
unadopted. Historical paths saying not-in-version3 retain their historical
meaning and do not exclude a subsequently reviewed Version 4 construction.

This is an explicit research selection, not every private working file.
Conversational transcripts, credentials, unrelated projects, raw private
archives and machine-local caches are excluded. Original research notes may
refer to omitted working paths. Consult the current source package and proof
inventory for portable dependencies. The historical baseline retains its
original notices and filtering policy; packaging it is not a new audit of its
content or redistribution rights.

The November 6 revision-2 snapshot includes twelve selected supplied mathematical documents and ledgers under `current/research/2026-11-06-collaborator-notes/supplied/`, plus the new comparison and height replacement. `SUPPLIED_SOURCES.json` records their exact hashes. `ARCHIVE_CATALOGUE.json` identifies all entries in the original ZIP and which were selected; central-directory metadata is not a content audit. The raw ZIP, nested ZIPs, prompts and execution logs are not republished.
