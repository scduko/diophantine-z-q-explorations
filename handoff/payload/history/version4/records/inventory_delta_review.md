## Target-two and the three latest deltas

**Target-two.** The prime-pattern numbers N_i are cardinalities of the
full E(F_p), with gcd(N_1,N_2)=4 and p not dividing N_1N_2. Good reduction,
odd characteristic and four distinct rational two-torsion points give
`|ker[2]|=4`, hence `|2E(F_p)|=N_i/4`. This elementary derived lemma
requires neither cyclicity nor surjectivity of 2E(K) onto the finite
image. Reduction containment suffices. No cross-prime coprimality is
introduced. These cardinality hypotheses belong to completeness; arbitrary
odd-contact soundness must not assume the prime was selected ordinary.

**Tangent deletion.** Its retained curve equations and the displayed
Bezout identity imply y nonzero and reconstruct the inverse with constant
denominator 4D^2. D has norm 49. This is exact algebra, including the
potential zero branches, not a new rank, rational-point, or nonsquareness
theorem. Inventory the full inverse-incidence/source binding separately.

**First-norm fusion.** Record two internal steps: the denominator change
has soundness and integer completeness, while `T=rW`,
`rN-r-T^2=0` is exact given the unchanged rational conic. That conic proves
r nonzero independently of deleted rows. At r=0 the isolated systems are
inequivalent. The new bound v(T)>=1 uses odd-valuation rounding; it does
not strengthen an external norm theorem. Polynomial dependence on theta
and real positivity preserve the existing pole completeness hypotheses.

**Mixed modes.** Make the clarification a required correction edge to
the affected frozen root/target-two proofs and reviews. The denominator
orders are e0=40,12,40 in full, beta-only, Lucas-only modes. Numerator
orders e_i satisfy e_i<=e0. With target multiplier c=2 (or historical 4),
use `v((h-c)/j_d)>=e0`, not a uniform 40 claim. This still makes h a unit
and gives `m_i/h=n_i mod p^e_i`. Stronger chosen completeness witnesses
do not prove a stronger statement for arbitrary beta-only witnesses.
No equation or external theorem changes.
