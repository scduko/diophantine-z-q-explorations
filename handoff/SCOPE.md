# Version 5 handoff selection

Date: November 6, 2026.

The current collection is the complete regular-file tree of one reviewed public commit. Plan preparation rejects forbidden paths, credentials, conversation markers, missing proof files, stale release counts or an incomplete reproduction receipt. It does not silently drop files from that commit.

The Version 5 package and dependency inventory are included through that complete current snapshot. A separate file manifest binds every package file. Its fresh reproduction receipt must contain all fourteen required successful stages and an exactly matching rebuilt polynomial. Generated replay directories are kept outside the repository and archives. Frozen historical pins additionally require the earlier quartics and original construction to retain their exact bytes.

The separate history selection contains project mathematical proofs, independent reviews and short machine-readable acceptance records. `selection.json` gives each original path, destination, scope, byte count and SHA-256. Existing Version 2 notes remain unchanged. New accepted-source notes and separate research use different directories and explicit scope descriptions.

`history/version4/` contains the selected three-mode, target-two, tangent, shared-square and first-norm research. Optional scalar/J0 fusion is explicitly marked unadopted. Historical Version 3 records retain their original paths and status, including alternatives which were not then adopted. The current release descriptor determines which construction is selected; a historical directory name does not determine current mathematical applicability.

No raw private archive, transcript, access file, credential or unrelated project is selected. The November 6 revision-2 snapshot adds the explicitly inventoried mathematical articles under `research/2026-11-06-collaborator-notes/`; the raw downloaded comparison ZIP and its nested archives are excluded. This is not a claim to preserve all research history. Original notes may refer to omitted context. Their older dates and provisional language remain as provenance; the current release descriptor and exact acceptance records identify the released result.

The full handoff preserves the already-public November 4 revision-2 baseline byte for byte. This packaging does not claim a new content audit or new redistribution rights for that historical collection. The baseline's notices and filtering remain in force.

The archive verifier checks integrity and current proof-package closure. The independent published ZIP or plan checksum authenticates the selected snapshot. Neither replaces mathematical review.
