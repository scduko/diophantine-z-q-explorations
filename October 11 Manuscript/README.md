# October 11 manuscript

This folder preserves the [October 11, 2026 manuscript PDF](ZIsDiophantineInQBySohailsGPT-6.pdf), *A conditional Diophantine definition of Z in Q from four arithmetic inputs*. The October 11 manuscript was produced by Sohail Farhangi with ChatGPT 6. The PDF is unchanged.

The current manuscript is [*Z is Diophantine in Q*, Version 2](../quartic_12504.pdf), with [TeX source](../quartic_12504.tex) and an [explicit quartic package](../quartics/quartic-12504/README.md). Version 2 is a collaboration using the collective agentic notes from Gabe Conant, Sohail Farhangi, James Freitag, Elliot Glazer, Jennifer Park, Caroline Terry. This credit does not change the October 11 manuscript's provenance.

## Scope and comparison

The October 11 argument explicitly assumes four arithmetic inputs: pole parity, rank one, a five-point height estimate, and compatible prime patterns. It uses approximation at the fixed prime 3 and leaves several constants and the final variable count unspecified. Its degree-four conversion therefore does not supply a numerical variable bound.

Version 2 presents an arithmetic proof and specifies a quartic in 12,504 total variables, with 12,503 rational witnesses. The [evidence notes](../docs/quartic-12504/README.md) distinguish its arithmetic dependencies from the finite certificate checks. The [repository verification status](../README.md#verification-status) records the limits of the reviews and the incomplete Lean formalization.

The [earlier comparison](https://github.com/elliotglazer/diophantine-z-q/blob/97cec7fdd757d9a8e4f468151a47a6d3610fb2f2/October%2011%20Manuscript/README.md) remains available at its exact historical commit. Its page counts, November 5 source hashes, and large-polynomial bounds describe that revision, not Version 2.
